# PSCoMiXX PostgreSQL Storage Audit & Optimization Plan

## Objective

Perform a complete audit of the PSCoMiXX platform and identify why PostgreSQL storage has grown to approximately 1361 GiB.

The goal is to:

1. Reduce PostgreSQL storage costs.
2. Prevent future storage-related billing surprises.
3. Establish a scalable architecture that can support the entire Press Start ecosystem.
4. Prepare the platform for long-term growth across education, comics, creator tools, streaming, portfolios, and community features.

---
