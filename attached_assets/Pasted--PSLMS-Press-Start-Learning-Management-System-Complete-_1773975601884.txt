## PLATFORM OVERVIEW

PSLMS is the **central brain** of the Press Start ecosystem. It's where all student data, XP, grades, portfolios, and progress converge from three connected apps:

1. **PSLMS** (this app) — pressstart.tech — Learning, grading, XP, portfolios
2. **CoMiXX** — pressstart.space — Comic/card/visual novel creation
3. **PSStreaming** — psstreaming.com — Video streaming & publishing

All three apps share the same API keys and match users by **email address**.

- **XP sources**:
  - Assignment completion (teacher awards per rubric)
