Build a school-safe “Promo Page + Ad Creator” system for PSCoMiXX Creator.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT build random ad injection.
Do NOT use targeted ads.
Do NOT track minors for advertising.
This is a controlled creative promo system for:
- platform promo pages
- educational sponsor pages
- creator-made fictional ad pages
- media literacy / marketing assignments

Goal:
Add comic-style promo/ad pages that can be used inside comic projects, exports, templates, and lessons while staying school-safe.

Core Feature Name:
Promo Page Studio

Main User Flow:
1. User opens Comic Creator
2. User can add a “Promo Page” between comic pages
3. User chooses from templates
4. User edits text, images, QR code, logo, offer/call-to-action
5. User exports comic with promo page included
6. For student accounts, only teacher/admin-approved templates can be used

Add these promo page types:

1. Platform Promo Page
Examples:
- “Create your next comic in CoMiXX”
- “Publish to PSStreaming”
- “Earn XP by finishing your project”
- “Join the Press Play Showcase”

2. Sponsor Page
Examples:
- Wacom-style tablet promo
- Reallusion-style character animation promo
- Unreal-style game dev promo
Must be clearly labeled:
“Sponsored Page”

3. Student/Fictional Ad Page
For classroom/media literacy assignments.
Examples:
- fake cereal ad
- fake superhero toy ad
- fake game release ad
- fake movie poster ad
Must be clearly labeled:
“Student-Created Promo”

4. Creator Promo Page
For creators promoting:
- their comic series
- their profile
- their marketplace pack
- their HOPs
- their channel

Required Safety Rules:
- Student accounts cannot receive targeted ads
- Student accounts only see platform/teacher-approved promo templates
- All sponsored pages must display “Sponsored Page”
- All user-created promo templates must display “Student-Created Promo” or “Creator Promo”
- No third-party tracking pixels
- No behavioral ad targeting
- Teacher/admin can disable promo pages entirely
- Teacher/admin can approve or reject templates
