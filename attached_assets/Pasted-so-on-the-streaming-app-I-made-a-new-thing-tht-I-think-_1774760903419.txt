Replit Build Brief: Add HOPs Creation System to PSCoMiXX

I need you to build a new creation flow into the PSCoMiXX / CoMiXX Creator platform for a content format called HOPs.

What HOPs are

HOPs are short-form looping media experiences designed to pull a user into a vibe, moment, scene, or musical loop.

A HOP can be:

a 30-second clip
a 90-second clip
a single image
a GIF
a short video clip
a micro-sequence / series of clips or images that play in order

The concept came out of the music/content side of the ecosystem. A lot of my music is built around 4–8 bar loops, so the point of a HOP is not just to “watch” it once, but to see how long a viewer stays inside the loop / mood / moment.

Core idea

A HOP should feel like a short looping audiovisual scene or story fragment that a creator can make quickly inside CoMiXX and then push to the Streaming platform.

The system should support:

creators building HOPs inside CoMiXX
previewing them before publish/export
packaging a single HOP or HOP series
sending them to the Streaming platform
tracking loop engagement / views / repeat plays
awarding XP tied to creation + publishing + engagement
Product Direction
Important

This does not necessarily need to be a full separate mode.

It may be better as:

a HOP Template
a HOP Builder workflow
or a Quick Create preset inside existing creation tools

I want the cleanest, fastest implementation that fits naturally into CoMiXX without bloating the app.

Think of HOPs as a lightweight creative format somewhere between:

motion comic panels
looped music visuals
micro visual novels
animated mood boards
short social media scenes
